Ngā Raina Matua e Whā o te Ringa

Ka aro nui te pānui ringa tuku iho ki ngā raina matua e whā i runga i te kapu ringa. Ko te Raina Ngākau, e tata ana ki te taha runga o te kapu ringa, e hono ana ki ngā kare-ā-roto me ngā whanaungatanga. Ko te Raina Oranga, e piko ana i te taha o te turanga o te koromatua, e hono ana ki te ora me ngā huringa o te oranga — ehara tēnei i te matapae roanga ora.
