Karatun Tafin Hannu na Gargajiya da AI

GetPalmAura wata manhajar yanar gizo ce da ke haɗa karatun tafin hannu na gargajiya da fasahar AI ta zamani. Loda hoto mai haske na tafin hannunka don samun cikakken bayani, mai kyan gani, game da layukan hannu, yatsu, tudu da alamomi na musamman. Gwada kuma Aura Camera dinmu don karatu irin na jiyya ta launi ta gargajiya. Manhajar tana tallafawa fiye da harsuna 99. Wannan manhaja don manufofin gargajiya da nishaɗi ne kawai kuma ba ta bayar da ganewar asali na likitanci ko hasashen kimiyya.

Manyan Layuka Guda Hudu na Hannu

Karatun tafin hannu na gargajiya yana ba da kulawa ta musamman ga manyan layuka hudu a tafin hannu. Layin Zuciya, kusa da saman tafin hannu, a al'adance yana da alaka da motsin rai da dangantaka. Layin Kai, wanda ke ratsa tsakiya, a al'adance yana da alaka da salon tunani da yanke shawara. Layin Rayuwa, wanda ke lankwasa kewayen tushen babban yatsa, a al'adance yana da alaka da kuzari da canje-canjen rayuwa — wannan ba hasashen tsawon rai ba ne. GetPalmAura yana duba dalla-dalla kowanne daga cikin waɗannan layuka a hotonka.
